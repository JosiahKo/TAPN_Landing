FAQ

Does TAPN read student content?

No. TAPN only manages policy state (which apps are allowed) and timestamps. We don’t capture messages, browsing, or photos.

What phones are supported?

iOS and Android. Students install a lightweight companion app that listens for the tap-in signal and applies school policies.

What if a student doesn’t tap in?

Attendance won’t log for that student. Schools can require tap-in for attendance or network access.
